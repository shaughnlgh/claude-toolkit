# Cold Read

Read back the unstated assumptions behind how I have been talking to you in this conversation. The blind-spot check: surface what I am taking for granted without realizing it. Invoke it when a direction feels obvious, or before committing to one, to catch the premises I never examined. Where `/toolkit:mirror-test` checks how my words land, this checks the premises underneath them.

## Usage

$ARGUMENTS

Arguments, if any, narrow the read to a specific decision or thread. With no arguments, read the whole conversation so far.

## What to do

Work only from what I have actually said in this conversation, not from what would be generically true of someone in my position. Infer from my word choices, what I treat as settled, what I never question, and what I skip over.

Surface the assumptions I do not know I am making:

1. **The load-bearing assumption.** The premise I am treating as fact without stating it, the one that reshapes the problem if it is wrong. Point to where in my messages it shows.
2. **The unexamined frame.** The way I have framed the problem that quietly rules out options I have not considered. Name an option the frame is hiding.
3. **The tell.** One concrete thing in how I phrased something that reveals an assumption I probably would not endorse if it were said out loud.

Be specific and point to my actual words. Do not flatter, and do not invent assumptions just to have something to say. If my premises are sound and already stated, say so plainly and stop.
